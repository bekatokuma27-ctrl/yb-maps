YB-Maps Help & Support

Mobile / Phone Navigation Guide:

  • Search: Type any city or country in top left bar to view results with flags and location details.
  • Map Views: Tap layers button to choose Default, Satellite, or Satellite with Names.
  • Directions & Routes: Open directions panel, click Start, then tap two locations on the map to calculate route.
  • Compass Alignment: Tap compass icon anytime to re-align map to exact North.

Desktop / Laptop Master Guide:

  • Fast Search Engine: Instant location searching showing city, country, flag, and details.
  • Pan & Zoom Controls: Use mouse scroll wheel or bottom zoom +/- buttons for smooth navigation.
  • Route Planning: Name your commute and save custom driving routes for quick access.
  • Home: Set your home on the map, then click Home to return to it.
  • Nearby Search: When Home is saved, search suggestions include restaurants, hotels, museums, pharmacies, and things to do around you.
  • Close Search Results: Clicking a search result now zooms closely to the selected place.

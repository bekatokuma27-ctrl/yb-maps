YB-Maps Help

Mobile / Phone Guide:

  • Search: Tap the search bar at the top left to find any location or address globally.
  • Zoom In & Out: Use zoom buttons (+/-) at the bottom.
  • Manage Layout: Switch between Default Map, Google Satellite, and Satellite with Names.
  • Directions & Routes: Name routes, click "Start", and tap map points.
  • Preferences: Customize fonts, language, or data persistence.

Desktop / Laptop Guide:

  • Search: Click top search bar and type to look up places.
  • Zoom In & Out: Use zoom buttons or mouse scroll wheel.
  • Manage Layout: Toggle map layers instantly from the top-left toolbar.
  • Directions & Routes: Define routes and manage saved paths.
  • Preferences: Access typography and language settings.
